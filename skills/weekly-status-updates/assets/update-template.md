# Weekly status update template

Written to be read on a phone, between other things, by someone who may not
remember what this project is. Every section earns its place by being the thing
that reader needs; anything else goes behind a link.

Where the inputs do not supply something, leave the labeled gap in position
rather than deleting the line. A deleted line reads as nothing to report, which
is a different claim from nobody knows.

missed a month can still act on the update.]
