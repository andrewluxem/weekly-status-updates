# Draft check scorecard

Run against a written update before it is sent. Each line is pass, fail, or not
applicable, and every fail names the location and the fix. The output is the
marked-up update plus this scorecard, never a silently rewritten update: the
author writes one of these every week, so what changed matters more than this
week's copy.

## The status call

- The status is one of the six defined values, not an invented one.
- Green appears only where every part of the goal is on schedule and every
  dependency is identified and scheduled.
- Yellow and red carry a revised date, or an explicit note that it is not yet
  known and what has to happen before it can be set.
- Complete and complete late carry a completion date, and complete late still
  shows the original date.
- The call carries one sentence of justification naming the specific part of the
  goal that is off schedule.

## Structure

- Title carries the project name, the week number, and the week-ending date.
- The overview is present and written for someone with no background, not
  shortened because the reader saw it last week.
- Highlights and lowlights are both present, or the absence of lowlights is
  deliberate rather than an omission.
- The milestone plan shows both what completed and what is next.
- Risks and dependencies are present, each with an owner or a labeled gap.
- Links point outward with a description of what the reader will find.

## Ownership and dates

- Every risk and dependency has an owner or a labeled gap in the owner position.
- No vague time reference survives: next week, soon, shortly, end of month, in
  the coming weeks. Each is an exact date or a labeled gap.
- Dates are mm/dd/yyyy.
- A supplied name is never replaced by a placeholder.

## Numbers and voice

- Every change states both sides and the unit, not just the direction.
- No figure appears that was not in the inputs.
- Active voice throughout.
- No weasel words: significantly, substantially, various, several, a number of,
  fairly, quite, arguably, it was decided.
- Progress is stated against the plan, not against effort. Worked on, spent time
  on, and made progress on are not statuses.

## The phone test

- The whole update is scannable on a phone. The status is visible without
  scrolling past the overview.
- A reader who has never heard of this project can say what it is for and
  whether it is in trouble.
